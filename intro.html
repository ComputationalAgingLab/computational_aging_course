Welcome to the Computational Biology of Aging coursebook#

Abstract#

This course is devoted to the study of key methods of computational biology of aging, which have had a significant impact on the field in recent years. We will take a detailed look at aging clock models, survival curves, Gompertz models, dynamical models as well as key aspects of the biology of aging (including its molecular part). Students will get experience in differential gene expression or methylation analysis in the context of aging, hazard ratio model fitting and interpretation, aging clocks construction and their exploration, and develop an intuition for dynamical systems modeling. The course aims to form a systematic view of the biology of aging, and offer a holistic computational methodology for the study of this problem.

Who is this course for?#

Any person who is interested in Aging Biology and has a basic technical or bioinformatics background.
